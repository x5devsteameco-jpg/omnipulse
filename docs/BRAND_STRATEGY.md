# Brand Naming Strategy — AEG Social Intelligence Platform

## 1. Brand Positioning Overview

### Core Value Propositions

| Capability | Value Delivered |
|------------|-----------------|
| Autonomous Multi-Platform Data Extraction | Harvests from 6 major social platforms without manual intervention |
| Real-Time Trending Analysis | Instant identification of emerging trends and conversation shifts |
| Predictive Exposure Forecasting | Anticipates content performance before posting |
| Marketing Gap Identification | Surfaces underserved audiences and missed opportunities with actionable recommendations |
| Follower/Viewer Metric Breakdown | Granular analytics across all audience segments |
| Statistical Confidence Scoring | Probability-weighted insights for decision-making |

### Target Audience Profile

| Segment | Primary Decision Drivers |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Social Media Marketing Teams | Efficiency, cross-platform consistency, actionable insights |
| Digital Agency Strategists | Scalability, client reporting, competitive intelligence |
| Brand Managers | Cross-platform presence tracking, ROI measurement |
| Growth-Focused CMOs | Data-driven decision support, market opportunity identification |

### Linguistic Directions

**Technical Register**
- Conveys precision, data-driven authority, algorithmic sophistication
- Appeals to analysts and metrics-focused professionals
- Keywords: metrics, analytics, data, insights, precision, algorithm

**Evocative Register**
- Creates emotional resonance and memorable imagery
- Appeals to creative directors and brand-focused strategists
- Keywords: pulse, signal, flow, trajectory, horizon

**Minimalist Register**
- Clean, direct, memorable understement
- Appeals to modern SaaS users and mobile-first decision makers
- Keywords: clear, fast, simple, direct

## 2. Six Name Proposals

### 1. PulseMap

**Elevator Pitch:** Visualize your social reach as a living map of audience engagement.

**Rationale:** Combines "pulse" (real-time activity, heartbeat of data) with "map" (visual representation of audience geography and connections). Communicates both the temporal nature of social intelligence and the spatial visualization of cross-platform presence. The compound word structure suggests mapping technology while remaining accessible.

**Linguistic Register:** Evocative with technical undertones

**Domain Availability:**
- PulseMap.com — Likely taken, suggests investigation
- PulseMap.io — Available
- PulseMap.ai — Premium tier, worth evaluating
